  1. Mix the warm water, sugar, and yeast in a bowl. Let it sit for 5–10 minutes until frothy.
  2. In a large bowl, combine flour and salt. Pour in the yeast mixture and olive oil. Stir until a shaggy dough forms.
  3. Cover and let the dough rise in a warm place for 1–2 hours until doubled in size.
  4. Oil a baking tray generously. Transfer the dough and stretch it to fill the tray. Drizzle more oil on top.
  5. Dimple the surface with your fingertips. Optionally top with flaky salt, rosemary, or olives.
  6. Let it rest for 20–30 minutes for a final rise.
  7. Bake at 220°C (425°F) for 20–25 minutes until golden brown on top.
  8. Remove from oven and let cool slightly before slicing. Enjoy!
